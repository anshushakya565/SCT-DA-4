# Marketing Campaign EDA & Business Insights Report

## Project Overview

This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a marketing campaign dataset to understand customer behavior, campaign effectiveness, purchasing trends, and marketing performance.

The primary objective of this project is to identify:

* Which customer segments generate higher spending
* Which marketing channels perform best
* Why campaign response rates are low
* Which strategies can improve marketing ROI

The project emphasizes business understanding and analytical storytelling rather than machine learning.

---

## Business Objective

The objective of this analysis is to evaluate customer purchasing behavior and campaign effectiveness to recommend marketing strategies and budget allocation improvements based on customer engagement and ROI-related insights.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation

* Checked missing values
* Handled null values using imputation techniques
* Removed duplicate rows
* Converted date columns into proper datetime format
* Created new analytical features such as:

  * Age
  * Total Spending
  * Total Purchases

---

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Performed analysis on:

* Customer demographics
* Income distribution
* Product category spending
* Marketing campaign responses
* Purchase channel performance
* Customer engagement trends

---

### 3. Funnel Analysis

A simplified marketing funnel was created using:

* Website Visits
* Purchases
* Campaign Responses

This helped analyze customer drop-off between engagement and campaign conversion stages.

---

## Key Insights

* Customers demonstrate strong purchasing activity through digital channels.
* Campaign response rates remain relatively low despite customer engagement.
* Higher-income customers contribute significantly higher spending.
* Wine and meat products generated the highest overall revenue.
* Web purchases dominate customer transactions, indicating strong online engagement.

---

## Business Recommendations

* Increase investment in digital marketing channels due to strong online purchasing activity.
* Improve campaign personalization using customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
* Focus marketing efforts on high-spending customer segments.
* Promote high-performing product categories through targeted campaigns.
* Improve campaign targeting strategies to increase response rates and ROI.
